MNI152NLin6Asym derived reference data
---------------------------------------
The target-subject sphere registrations, SUMA surfaces, M2M maps, and ribbon
masks were generated locally by running FreeSurfer recon-all and AFNI/SUMA on
an MNI152NLin6Asym template. They are not official TemplateFlow surface files.
Confirm the source template's license and attribution terms before publishing
or mirroring these derived files.
